*FitFlow AI Service

This folder is reserved for the Python-based AI microservice proposed for the FitFlow redesign.

*Planned Technology

-Python
-FastAPI
-Machine learning models

*Main Responsibilities

The AI service is planned to support:

-Personalised workout plan generation
-Nutrition and food recognition
-AI-assisted recommendations

The AI service will be accessed internally through the NestJS backend rather than directly by the mobile application.
